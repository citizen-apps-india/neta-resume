A draft roll is not the final roll — names still return through claims and appeals.
Why draft &ne; final
A state's draft roll is published soon after enumeration. Names left off it can still come back through claims and objections, and new electors are added, before the final roll is published — so the share left off the draft usually runs higher than the eventual net change. Where a state's final roll isn't in the record yet, only the draft measure can be shown.
