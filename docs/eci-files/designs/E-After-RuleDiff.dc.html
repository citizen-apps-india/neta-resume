About this text

Neither side is from a primary document. The statutory Form 6 could not be fetched: the ECINET portal serves only the 2022 Form 6 guidelines at voters.eci.gov.in/guidelines/Form-6_en.pdf, not the form. The ‘before’ lines paraphrase The Indian Express's description of the statutory form. The three options on the ‘after’ side are the portal's wording as The Indian Express quoted it; the other ‘after’ lines paraphrase its report. The online form sits behind a login and could not be opened. The report says the question was not marked mandatory, but the form could not be submitted without answering it.
