What makes it hard to use

  1. 1
    Three long cards of prose, no comparisonRules read as paragraphs; nothing shows who sits on each panel at a glance.
  2. 2
    Circles with no labelsPM, LoP and CJI markers are unlabelled dots; you must already know the rule.
  3. 3
    Citations before the storyEach card ends in a wall of citation links before a single selection appears.
  4. 4
    The eight selections start off-screenThe real timeline of appointments begins only after three full-height cards.
  5. 5
    No line from panel to appointeeNothing connects who chose to who was chosen, or flags a recorded dissent.
