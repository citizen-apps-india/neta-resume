What makes it hard to use

  1. 1
    Three sentences before any visualA paragraph explains the three columns instead of showing them; nothing here answers the page's question at a glance.
  2. 2
    The charge column dwarfs the restA full paragraph, a “Read in full” link and an italic note make the charge four times the height of its answer.
  3. 3
    “No response on record” is a caption, not an answerRendered small and grey next to a full paragraph, the two sides of the same row read as unequal in importance.
  4. 4
    A third column that is usually empty“What the record shows” gets a whole column, but only 14 of 61 rows have anything in it — mostly dead space.
  5. 5
    Chips count the record; nothing charts it“All 61 / No response on record 27” are numbers with no chart, so the by-year shape of the answer never shows.
