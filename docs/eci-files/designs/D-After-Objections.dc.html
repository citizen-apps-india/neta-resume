Warns of 'gradual centralisation' of roll database

Vivek Joshi wrote to the CEC, Sandhu and Deputy Election Commissioner Sanjay Kumar about gradual centralisation of the electoral roll database and proposed two committees, including an outside IIT expert, to certify that only statutory authorities can change the database and that ECINet complies with the law.

What followed: Sandhu endorsed the proposal on 13 July 2026; on 14 August he recorded that he knew of no action taken on it.
